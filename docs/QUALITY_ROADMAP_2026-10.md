# MotorsportVerse quality priorities — October 2026

This is a proposed sequence. Existing foundations include the series catalog,
shared model/data packages, race and driver pages, standings, probability layers
and evaluation/promotion tooling. Readiness differs by series.

Reference: [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/) makes schedule, results,
standings, drivers and teams first-class destinations. Apply a race-weekend
hierarchy to RaceIQ while retaining MotorsportVerse's assets.

1. **Establish publication integrity.** This pass keeps reported F2 retirements
   in win/podium scores and labels replays. Next, freeze pre-weekend and
   post-qualifying snapshots by version; separate historical exports and
   published forecasts; expose source coverage and last completed round.
   Acceptance: missing results yield no invented labels, future calibrators
   cannot rewrite published snapshots and skipped probes cannot imply freshness.
2. **Evaluate race types temporally.** Fit pace/skill/calibration on prior
   weekends and hold a final season out. Pair probability and rank baselines
   on the same eligible entrants, report denominators and separate sprint,
   feature, circuit and weather strata. Include reported DNF negatives.
   Promote a new head only with paired evidence and calibration checks; an F1
   measurement does not establish another series' quality.
3. **Make the weekend the browsing unit.** Lead with selected/next event,
   session schedule and pre-race/post-qualifying state. Connect results,
   driver/team pages and championship scenarios; preserve filters/back context.
   Acceptance: mobile/keyboard calendar → event → driver flows retain context;
   stale/synthetic/missing sources are readable in text; evidence is nearby.

Immutable publication and source status precede more series/model complexity.
No new benchmark, live accuracy or completed redesign is claimed here.
